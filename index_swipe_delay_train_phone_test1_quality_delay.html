Swipe Delay & Video Quality Evaluation

Goal: This evaluation aims to measure the impact of swipe delay and video quality on user's Quality of Experience in short video service. During the evaluation, you will watch multiple test sample, each consists of a list of six short videos. When a pop-up appears on the video, please swipe down on your screen to go to next video in the list. Delay or change in Video quality may occur when swiping to the next video. At the end of each session, you will be asked to rate the quality of the test sample on a 5-point scale as follows.
  1. Poor
  2. Bad
  3. Fair
  4. Good
  5. Excellent
Procedure:
  1. Click the "Start Training Session" button below to start a training session. In the training session, you will be showing several test samples in which delay or change in video quality will be displayed. You can also practice choosing score for each test sample. At the end of the training session, you will be directed to the main evaluation.
  2. At the beginning of the main evaluation session, you will be asked to provide your name, age, and experience with short video service. The main evaluation session consists of total 41 test samples. The evaluation time is approximately 15 minutes. At the end of the evaluation, please download a log file containing all of your evaluation scores and upload it to the specified drive folder.
